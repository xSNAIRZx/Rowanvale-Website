# Start here — Rowanvale 18.4.0 RC

Open Games & Media Hub. Your five media formats share one collection and search. The expandable directory links below the shelves retain the original full tools.

**Add a story:** choose its format and title. Offline matches appear as you type. Online search is optional; its checkbox explains that only title and category leave the device. Choose a result, inspect its cover, synopsis, year and edition, and confirm the match. Use manual entry for anything missing. Provider page counts are suggestions, never a replacement for your edition's count.

**Details:** edit a synopsis, upload a cover, choose a rating, keep notes, find a different catalog match or save a permitted cover offline. To deliberately replace an existing manual synopsis or custom cover with a provider result, check the separate replacement permission box. Batch maintenance cannot replace protected fields.

**Progress:** log pages, episodes, season, game hours or completion. Correcting new activity reconciles related XP; restoring the same activity restores its original award without creating another one. The original 18.3 XP balance is retained.

**For you:** unfinished stories, favorites, goals and optional restorative activities. Enable voluntary mood use only if you want it. Hide formats, dismiss a suggestion, show less of its tags, or reset preferences. Recommendations work offline and are never a diagnosis or treatment.

**Sessions:** start from a story. Reminders are off until enabled, and only appear in the Sessions panel. Choose frequency and reminder kinds. Finish with an optional reflection and correct idle time. A short session has no XP requirement. A session lasting at least five minutes can earn a reward once per story per day; chosen breaks can earn a daily wellness reward. Resting has no penalty.

**Artwork & details:** choose missing fields, refresh, or broken artwork; preview up to twelve stories. Confirm only correct matches before applying. Cancel and retry failed jobs. Duplicates are reported for your review, never deleted automatically. Online lookup needs your preference consent and configured sources. The queue is kept in memory; reopening the app starts a new maintenance session.

**Your journey:** existing overall Rowanvale level plus eight optional specialization tracks. Future rewards can be changed from 0 to 100. Repeatable activities share a 300 XP daily ceiling; all new awards share a 750 XP daily ceiling. Limits prevent repeated edits or timer clicks from creating unlimited XP. Reading awards cover the first 500 pages; episode backfill awards cover the first 500 episodes per season.

**Backups:** export before upgrading or deleting records. JSON contains the workspace. Use the existing portable ZIP artwork backup for selected covers stored separately on disk. Browser offline artwork copies are limited to the latest 80 requested selected covers; the local server retains its normal larger private cache.
