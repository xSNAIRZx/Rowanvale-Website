# Hearthbyte brand and copyright checklist

This note is included for project administration. It is general information, not legal advice or a statement that the Hearthbyte name or logo is available or registered.

## Proposed copyright notice

`© 2026 Hearthbyte. All rights reserved.`

Use this only for original project code, text, and artwork to the extent those rights are owned by the person or entity using the notice. If rights are owned by a different person or organization, replace `Hearthbyte` with the actual legal rights-holder. Keep a record of contributors, original source files, licenses, and any assignments of rights.

## Trademark use

- `Hearthbyte` signals that a mark is being claimed; it does **not** mean the mark is federally registered.
- Do not use `®` unless the mark is registered for the relevant goods/services in the jurisdiction where the symbol is used.
- This project has not completed trademark clearance. Search for exact and similar names, logos, and pronunciations, including related software, games, entertainment, wellness, and fitness services.
- Determine the intended owner and relevant goods/services, and get appropriate legal advice before a commercial launch. Trademark protection is tied to particular uses and jurisdictions.
- Official starting points: [USPTO Trademark Basics](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics), [USPTO trademark search](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search).

## Third-party material

Game names, logos, provider data, fonts, libraries, and externally sourced content may have their own terms. This notice does not claim ownership of third-party marks or assets. Keep required attributions and comply with each source's API/license terms.

## Copyright reference

The U.S. Copyright Office explains that original software and expressive site content may be protected, while names, titles, slogans, facts, ideas, and methods are generally not protected by copyright alone: [What Does Copyright Protect?](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-protect.html).


## Working brand status

Hearthbyte is the working name used in this preview build. A preliminary web search of other candidate names found several existing products/marks, so do not treat this name or the new logo concept as legally cleared. Before public or commercial launch, search the USPTO database for identical and confusingly similar word and design marks across relevant goods/services, check other jurisdictions and domains, and consider trademark counsel. Do not use ® unless the relevant mark is registered.
